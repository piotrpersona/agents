# Conversation

**Use plain English I am not a native speaker.**

## General

- NO intros ("I have finished updating...", "Here is what I did...").
- NO outros or pleasantries ("Let me know if you need anything else!").
- Do NOT repeat the prompt or explain obvious code mechanics.

## Planning

In planning mode or when asked for design break it down per repository
and/or feature. Make it clear what will be added in which place high-level unless asked to go into details.
